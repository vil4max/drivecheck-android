Drive Check for Android: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Drive Check (“the App”) shows the air raid alert status of the region of Ukraine you are in. It is published on Google Play by vil4max.

Location

The App asks for your location only while it is open on screen, to find which region to show. It never asks for background location. To turn coordinates into a region, the App uses Android’s built-in geocoder, which may send the coordinates to the geocoding service of your device’s provider (on most devices, Google). The App itself never sends your location to the developer or to the alert data provider. If you do not allow location, the App shows Kyiv.

Alert data

When you open the App, refresh it, or a widget or notification updates, the App requests the public alert status of all regions from the Ubilling aerial alerts service (ubilling.net.ua). The Status tab can also load the provider’s alert map image. These requests carry no personal data; like any web request, they reveal your IP address and an app identifier (“DriveCheck” and its version) to that service.

Notifications, widget and quick-settings tile

The alert notification, the home-screen widget and the quick-settings tile are created on your device from the same alert data. You can turn the notification off in the App’s Details tab or in Android settings.

What the App keeps

The App keeps the last alert data, your current region and its own settings on your device only. They are not backed up and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

What the App does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No ads and no advertising identifier.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDK.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

Google Play may give the developer aggregated install statistics and crash reports (Android vitals). They do not identify you.

Informational only

Alert data is informational. Do not rely on it for critical safety decisions; follow official instructions and local authorities.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
